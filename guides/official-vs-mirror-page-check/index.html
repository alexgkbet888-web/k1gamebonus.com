Copied branding is not proof

A mirror page can copy logo, colors, and app screenshots. Stronger evidence comes from stable domain behavior, support visibility, and consistent page structure.

Redirect behavior

If a download or login button changes domains without explanation, treat the path as higher risk and record the final URL.

Compare version details

Version tables, file-size notes, and article update dates can help identify whether a page is maintained or just copied.

Action checklist

  1. Check source domain before clicking.
  2. Record whether buttons redirect.
  3. Look for About, Contact, Terms, Privacy, and Support.
  4. Compare version and app-size notes where present.

FAQ

Can a mirror page look official?

Yes. Visual design can be copied, so source behavior and trust pages matter.

What should I record from a suspicious mirror?

Source URL, final redirect URL, download warning, and visible support information.

Is a version table enough to trust a page?

No. It is one signal, not a full trust decision.
