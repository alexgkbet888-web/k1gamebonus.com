Why is a domain change risky?

A changed domain does not automatically mean fraud, but it does increase uncertainty. Users should look for consistency across contact, support, privacy, terms, and visible product naming. A new link with no supporting pages or a mismatched brand trail deserves more caution than a copied homepage banner alone.

Evidence to keep before the next step

Capture the old domain, the new domain, and any message that directed users there. Then compare whether the support wording, privacy page, and account path still match. This is more useful than relying on a Telegram forward or a single agent screenshot.
